About Me

The Journey of a Mission-Driven Developer

From Passion to Purpose

My journey into technology began in 2018, driven by a simple yet powerful belief: technology should empower everyone, not just a select few. Over the past 7 years, I've transformed that belief into reality through code, innovation, and relentless dedication.

What started as curiosity about how things work evolved into a mission to build systems that make a real difference in people's lives. Every line of code I write, every product I launch, is a step toward democratizing technology and creating opportunities for millions.

The KRAITIN Vision

In founding KRAITIN, I set out to create more than just a company—I wanted to build an entire ecosystem that empowers people through technology, education, and innovation. KRAITIN represents my commitment to making advanced tech accessible and useful for everyday challenges.

Building LaunchLens

LaunchLens emerged from observing a critical gap in the startup ecosystem: founders lacked comprehensive tools to track, analyze, and optimize their product launches. By combining AI with real-time analytics across Product Hunt, X (Twitter), and other platforms, LaunchLens helps startups make data-driven decisions during their most critical moments.
